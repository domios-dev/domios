The labelling scheme

Every label on the board is produced by labels.py. An agent is labelled by what it is: the Orchestrator and the Planner by their team role, a worker by its task's stored executor_role, matched against the families below in order. A role that matches nothing is not swallowed: it is labelled Other with the raw role beside it, on the board and here.
