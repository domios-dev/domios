Which figures are worth acting on The test for every one: what would a person do differently after reading it. A figure that changes no action is a number, and numbers go behind the door. On a card the big number is the conclusion, so a figure that has to be subtracted, compared or divided before it means anything never leads: the oldest wait leads review, not the count; agents stopped leads agents, not the ratio; the projection leads runway, not today's percentage.
