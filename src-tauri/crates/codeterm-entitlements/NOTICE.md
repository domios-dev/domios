What happens if you do:

- **Your license ends.** Under the Violations section of the license, your right to use Domios terminates, and every copy you run after that is unlicensed.

Verdicts are signed with keys that never leave our servers. A patched build gains nothing it can keep, and it identifies the account that ran it.
