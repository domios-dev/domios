## Verify live

Merged is not live. Open the deployed page or listing yourself on desktop and phone, in light and dark: the right asset shows, copy matches the sheet, badges are static and correct, and the page has no errors. A deploy that did not trigger is caught only this way.

- No vote requests, paid votes or paid promotion.
