Every created agent, shell, and schedule belongs to its Team; close finalizes members, attempts, lanes, and crons. Plan work defaults to authored task dispatch; unplanned Workers are only for small, urgent, or exploratory work and still need a Team task. Diagnose blocked dispatch or mark it blocked; never replace it with a loose roleless agent.

Bind utility shells, schedules, and helpers to the Team. Standalone tabs or crons require the user's request. Helpers never replace task dispatch. Planners without spawn authority route requests (role, reason, owned work) to their Orchestrator. See `codeterm docs ORCHESTRATION-OPERATIONS` for commands and cleanup.
