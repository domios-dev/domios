Mission Control Board concepts

Three directions for the owner's running plan, each drawn at the real 980px Board width and at 390px with CodeTerm's github-dark tokens and the current Board data. Spend reads top-down in the owner's order: who took part, what each provider spent, tool calls, allowance spent, total, then models. A dashed chip such as needs new data marks a reading the data cannot give yet; it never shows a number. Plan Tokens and Turns are drawn with sample values, because no ledger row carries a plan id yet.

A. Needs you first Recommended

Questions, in order

  1. Is anything waiting on me right now?
  2. Is the plan moving, and how well?
  3. What has this machine spent: which providers, how much allowance, what total, which models?
  4. What happened most recently?

Shows, merges, drops

  • Open gate and held reviews become one Needs you panel carrying the question itself; it disappears when nothing waits.
  • Seven task statuses become the non-zero ones plus one quiet line for the rest; plan tokens and turns join Progress.
  • Bounces and done first try share a Quality pair.
  • Dropped: the grid of eight KPI cards, Longest wait (folded into the gate), the separate Held card.

Treatment

  • Every section is a raised panel with a ruled header in the sidebar's own section-label voice; nothing sits loose on the page.
  • Spend is a labelled story in four steps down one left rail: Took part, Allowance, Total, By model.
  • Each provider line carries its own units; allowance is points spent over the plan beside the level used now.
  • Only one graphic: a composition strip for the model split.

B. Plan beside machine

Questions, in order

  1. Where does the plan stand?
  2. What needs a decision?
  3. What has this machine spent, provider by provider, then in total, then by model?
  4. What happened?

Shows, merges, drops

  • Two panels with a hard boundary: the plan on the left, machine spend on the right, so plan scope and machine scope never mix.
  • The plan panel is a sidebar-style list of groups: status, decisions, this plan, quality, roles.
  • Dropped: every stand-alone KPI card and every zero status line.

Treatment

  • Looks like the CodeTerm sidebar grown into panels: uppercase group labels, rows with hover, hairline row rules.
  • Spend has no graphic at all. Groups run Took part, Allowance spent, Total, Models; every row is a label and a right-aligned value.

C. Timeline with a rail

Questions, in order

  1. What is happening, as a story?
  2. What is the one thing waiting on me? (pinned on top of the story)
  3. Where do the numbers stand? (rail: plan, then spend)

Shows, merges, drops

  • The event log becomes the main column; the open gate is pinned above it.
  • The rail carries a short plan summary and Spend in the owner's order.
  • Dropped: Tasks by role (reachable in the Tasks view), the KPI grid, Longest wait and Held as cards.

Treatment

  • Two containers only: one place for the story and one for the numbers.
  • Spend steps are stacked key and value lists; the model split closes with a donut and a legend grouped by provider.
  • At 390 the rail comes first, then the story.
