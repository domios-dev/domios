status: pending
---

Build one role abstraction without legacy worker-role hardcoding. Pane roles and task executor-role briefs should use the same RoleDescriptor DTO under the hood. A normal new agent pane is roleless. A pane becomes Orchestrator only through an explicit Make Orchestrator promotion flow that stores RoleDescriptor(kind=orchestrator) and injects onboarding. A worker is not a saved role/persona; it is an attached execution pane whose concrete role brief comes from the assigned task. Tasks may carry free-form executor role guidance generated by the orchestrator, plus optional built-in preset hints. For 1.6.2 ship only built-in Orchestrator as a persistent pane role template; do not build a custom role-template registry yet.
