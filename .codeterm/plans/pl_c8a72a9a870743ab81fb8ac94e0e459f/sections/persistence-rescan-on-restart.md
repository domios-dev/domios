A critical bug: plans on disk were not re-indexed after a daemon restart — the list only filtered the in-memory map and never scanned the directory, and watchers were not retained for pre-existing project dirs. Fix: list-by-project lazily scans <project>/.codeterm/plans, startup scans open-workspace CWDs, watchers cover scanned dirs. Plans now survive restart; verified by restarting dev and reloading the board with no re-seed.
