## 2. What stays on your machine

Domios runs locally. Your prompts, messages, terminal output, files, paths, workspace and tab names, memory, sessions and credentials stay on your machines. We do not receive them, except the limited installation and analytics data listed in Section 3.

You can object to account and purchase events at any time by writing to us.

Account data is required to create an account and buy a plan; without it you can still use Domios on the Free plan. Analytics are optional. If you are below the age of digital consent in your country (13 to 16 in the EU/EEA, 13 in the UK), turn analytics off unless your parent or guardian agrees.

Domios collects and sends no crash reports. Crash logs stay on your machine.

Payment details such as card numbers and billing address are collected by Paddle as Merchant of Record under Paddle's privacy policy. We do not receive card numbers.

## 6. Your rights

You may access, rectify, erase, restrict or object to processing of your data, receive it in a portable form, and withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier processing. Where we rely on legitimate interests (server logs, support and abuse prevention), you may object at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation. Contact g.andry90@gmail.com. You can complain to the Personal Data Protection Service of Georgia (<https://personaldata.ge>), and, if you are in the EU/EEA or UK, to the supervisory authority where you live or work (in the UK, the ICO).

### Deleting your account

To delete your account, email g.andry90@gmail.com from the address on the account and ask for deletion. We will delete your account and all personal data associated with it, including your entitlement and installation records, and confirm within one month of your request (GDPR Art. 12(3)); we may ask you to verify that the account is yours. Records that tax law requires us to keep, and payment data held by Paddle as an independent controller, are retained only as the law requires. Any remaining copies in backups are deleted in the normal backup cycle. Data on your own machines is not affected.
