Which deployment strategy would you like to use?

❯ 1. Blue/Green
     Run two identical environments; switch all traffic from the old (blue) to the new (green) at once. Instant rollback
     by switching back.
  2. Canary
     Roll out to a small subset of users/servers first, monitor, then gradually increase to 100%. Limits blast radius of
     bad releases.
  3. Rolling
     Incrementally replace old instances with new ones in batches. No extra environment needed, but rollback is slower.
