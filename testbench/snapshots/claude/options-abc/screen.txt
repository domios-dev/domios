⏺ A good case is when you ask me to do something and there are a few distinct, mutually exclusive ways to proceed — none
  clearly correct without your input. For example, if you said "set up linting for this project" and I found no config,
  I'd ask:

  How should I configure the linter?

  A) Strict — enable the recommended ruleset plus type-aware rules, and fail CI on any warning.
  B) Balanced — recommended ruleset only, warnings allowed locally but errors block CI.
  C) Minimal — just formatting/whitespace rules, no opinionated style enforcement.

  Each option leads to a genuinely different setup, so picking one changes what I build — that's the signal that a
  choice is worth surfacing rather than just defaulting.
